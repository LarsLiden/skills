3. Choose sessions

Connect your bot to manage sessions.

Opt-in shares the session name, question text and answer choices with Telegram. Bot chats are not end-to-end encrypted. Only select sessions whose questions are suitable for Telegram. Idle notifications also share the latest assistant reply when enabled. Full conversations, reasoning, files and tool arguments are not forwarded.

4. Session topics

Enable topics for your existing bridge bot in Telegram first, then enable them in this panel. No new bot, token or pairing is needed. Your existing messages stay where they are.

  1. In Telegram, open the official @BotFather conversation not your bridge bot's conversation.
  2. Tap BotFather's name at the top to open its profile. Tap Open App or Launch App to open BotFather's management mini app. This is not Configure Mini App in the chat's Bot Settings menu; that configures a mini app for your own bot.
  3. In the management app, select your existing bridge bot. Find its topic/thread settings and enable topics in private chats. Labels vary by Telegram version; look for Threads Settings or Threaded Mode, not necessarily those exact labels.
  4. Return to this connected, paired bridge panel and click Enable session topics below. The bridge checks Telegram's reported support, then creates a Bridge topic and one topic per selected session.
  5. On your phone, return to Telegram's chat list and reopen your bridge bot's conversation to see the topics. If the old mixed view remains, reopen it again; do not recreate topics just to refresh the view.
Alternative: BotFather's chat menu (not available in every version)

Send /mybots to @BotFather, select your existing bridge bot, then look under Bot Settings > Threads Settings > Threaded Mode. Turn it on if present. If Threads Settings is missing, use BotFather's profile and Open App or Launch App as described above, not Configure Mini App. Then return here to enable session topics.

Telegram's official private-chat topics guide confirms the BotFather requirement; its documentation does not specify exact menu labels.

Deselecting a session permanently deletes its Telegram topic and all messages. Confirmed archived/deleted Copilot sessions are also cleaned up automatically. Idle or temporarily unavailable sessions are kept; Bridge is never deleted. Sync session topics retries only explicitly rejected topic operations. Uncertain creation, rename or deletion outcomes require inspection and are not retried automatically.

On your phone

Read the full numbered choices in the question, then tap the matching Choose 1, Choose 2, etc. button, or use Telegram's Reply on the original question. Send /status to see selected sessions, or /tell 042 Your instruction in Bridge for a new message. Replace 042 with the session's three-digit code, including leading zeros.

Inside a session topic, just type your message or reply to a non-question message. No /tell prefix is required. /status shows that session; in Bridge it shows all selected sessions. Continue to answer questions using their buttons or Telegram Reply, not unaddressed text. A code for another session is rejected inside a session topic.

If Telegram omits the destination, or you type in Bridge, choose a session button for that new message. Nothing is sent until you choose. Cancel discards only that unsent message, not an agent's work. Unsent text is kept only in the hub's memory while connected and its recent receipt is retained; disconnecting or restarting discards it. Old buttons cannot resend it.

After a phone message or question answer, the first public reply is sent as soon as captured (* REPLY *); the final result still follows at idle when idle notices are enabled. Reload the extension in this hub and monitored sessions to enable first-reply capture.

Plans, permissions and forms stay in Copilot. Unsupported prompts produce an alert when exposed by the app; tool-permission alert coverage is not yet qualified. This bridge never enables blanket approvals.

Keep this hub session and the computer running. Closing or reopening this panel does not disconnect the bot. After an app/session restart, use Connect saved bot. Your bot, phone pairing and session selections are retained; old queued commands are discarded.
