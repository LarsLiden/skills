Only do this to replace your saved bot. Saving a replacement disconnects the current bot and clears phone pairing and session selections. To reconnect the same bot, cancel and use Connect saved bot. Never paste tokens into Copilot chat.

3. Choose sessions

Connect your bot to manage sessions.

Opt-in shares the session name, question text and answer choices with Telegram. Bot chats are not end-to-end encrypted. Only select sessions whose questions are suitable for Telegram. Idle notifications also share the latest assistant reply when enabled. Full conversations, reasoning, files and tool arguments are not forwarded.

Idle does not prove success: the agent may be finished or blocked. After installing the listener, reload extensions in the monitored sessions once, or restart the app. It captures future replies only; earlier messages are not replayed.

4. Session topics

In BotFather, enable private-chat topics for your existing bot. Then use Enable session topics here. The bridge creates a Bridge topic and one topic per selected session. Your existing messages stay where they are; no new bot or pairing is needed.

Deselecting a session permanently deletes its Telegram topic and all messages. Confirmed archived/deleted Copilot sessions are also cleaned up automatically. Idle or temporarily unavailable sessions are kept; Bridge is never deleted. Sync session topics retries only explicitly rejected topic operations. Uncertain creation, rename or deletion outcomes require inspection and are not retried automatically.

On your phone

Tap a choice or use Telegram's Reply on the original question. Send /status to see selected sessions, or /tell 042 Your instruction in Bridge for a new message. Replace 042 with the session's three-digit code, including leading zeros.

Inside a session topic, just type your message or reply to a non-question message. No /tell prefix is required. /status shows that session; in Bridge it shows all selected sessions. Continue to answer questions using their buttons or Telegram Reply, not unaddressed text. A code for another session is rejected inside a session topic.

After a phone message or question answer, the first public reply is sent as soon as captured (* REPLY *); the final result still follows at idle when idle notices are enabled. Reload the extension in this hub and monitored sessions to enable first-reply capture.

Plans, permissions and forms stay in Copilot. Unsupported prompts produce an alert when exposed by the app; tool-permission alert coverage is not yet qualified. This bridge never enables blanket approvals.

Keep this hub session and the computer running. Closing or reopening this panel does not disconnect the bot. After an app/session restart, use Connect saved bot. Your bot, phone pairing and session selections are retained; old queued commands are discarded.
